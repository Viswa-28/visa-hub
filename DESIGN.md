## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of sovereign legal authority and aerodynamic fluidity. Designed for international travelers, students, business leaders, and families navigating complex immigration pathways, the aesthetic projects unyielding institutional trust backed by effortless modern technology.

The design movement merges **Glassmorphism** with **Corporate Luxury Precision**:

- **Atmospheric Depth:** Frosted glass panels, crystalline translucent card layers, and ethereal sky-inspired linear gradients evoke the high-altitude optimism of global aviation.
- **Institutional Weight:** Crisp architectural structures, pristine borders, and refined typographical hierarchies instill absolute confidence in consular compliance and high approval rates (31k+ approvals).
- **Dynamic Feedback:** Subtle micro-interactions, live eligibility progress indicators, and tactile telemetry badges mimic high-end flight decks and state-of-the-art passport biometric interfaces.

## Colors

The color architecture communicates sovereign security balanced by modern digital velocity:

- **Primary (`#071B3B` - Deep Trust Navy):** The bedrock of legal authority, formal consular documentation, and high-contrast typography. Used across top-tier headers, major navigation bars, and primary operational anchors.
- **Secondary (`#00D2FF` - Electric Cyan):** The vibrant visual beacon representing real-time telemetry, active state indicators, flight paths, and spotlight interactive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB` - Royal Atlantic Blue):** Connects the authoritative deep navy with the high-energy cyan, providing steady contrast for primary action buttons, focused controls, and brand trust elements.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Refined cool greys utilized for secondary metadata, label legends, passport guidelines, and subtle grid dividers.

### Atmospheric Surfaces

- Canvas Background: Smooth atmospheric off-white (`#F8FAFC`) fading subtly into radiant sky tinting (`#EFF6FF`).
- Frosted Card Substrates: Pure white layers with translucent opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) paired with crisp border rings (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`).

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering wide geometric proportion, clean terminals, and executive clarity across both dense regulatory tables and expansive hero declarations.

- **Hero & Display Scales:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.02em to -0.03em) maximize visual punch and create clear authority in destination headers, stats panels, and lead value statements.
- **Data & Consular Labels:** `label-caps` is styled with uppercase transform and wide tracking (+0.08em), matching passport security strips, official document IDs, and status trackers.
- **Legibility Rules:** Standard body copy retains open line heights (1.5x) to maintain effortless readability over documents, application criteria, and legal disclaimers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum desktop container width of 1280px to guarantee optical balance on ultra-wide screens.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 32px (`gutter-lg`) spacing and 48px (`margin-desktop`) canvas padding. Complex interactive components—such as the Visa Fee & Eligibility Calculator—span multi-column asymmetrical cards (8 columns calculator / 4 columns real-time summary receipt).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 24px (`gutter`) spacing and 32px (`margin`) margins. Multi-step widgets stack into balanced vertical modules.
- **Mobile (&lt; 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`gutter-sm`) spacing and 20px (`margin-mobile`) outer margins. Search selectors, date pickers, and destination carousels convert to single-column or horizontal edge-swipe containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid of glassmorphism and subtle directional lighting that simulates sunlight through airplane cabin windows:

- **Level 0 (Atmosphere Canvas):** Base sky gradient layer (`#F8FAFC` to `#EFF6FF`) with ambient vector contours evoking global flight trajectories.
- **Level 1 (Frosted Glass Panels):** `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);` outlined by a subtle perimeter ring (`border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`). Casts a faint ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(7, 27, 59, 0.06);`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards & Popovers):** Elevated search panels and country selector cards. Enhanced shadow profile: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(7, 27, 59, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Active Telemetry Panels):** High-priority consular alerts and document upload overlays. Supported by an atmospheric backdrop overlay (`rgba(7, 27, 59, 0.4)` blur 8px) with a structured lift: `box-shadow: 0 25px 60px -12px rgba(7, 27, 59, 0.25);`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a standard roundedness setting of **`2` (Rounded)**, infusing friendly travel accessibility without compromising institutional rigor.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** 0.5rem (8px) radius ensures clean, ergonomic tap targets.
- **Cards & Modal Containers (`rounded-lg`):** 1rem (16px) radius for search widgets, visa requirement cards, and feature blocks.
- **Hero Cockpit & Floating Hub Surfaces (`rounded-xl`):** 1.5rem (24px) radius gives large focal components an aerodynamic contour.
- **Pill Badges & Tabs:** Full circle / capsule border-radius (`9999px`) reserved specifically for approval status badges, trust markers ("31k+ Approvals"), and primary segmented switches.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Navy (`#071B3B`) with crisp white typography, subtle glossy highlight at top edge (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`), rounding 8px. On hover: shifts into Royal Blue (`#2563EB`) with a delicate cyan ambient aura.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Clear frosted glass background with deep navy text and a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). On hover: background fills with `rgba(239, 246, 255, 0.8)`.
- **Cyan Accent (Telemetry / Instant Action):** Vibrant Cyan (`#00D2FF`) base with dark navy text (`#071B3B`) for urgent conversion buttons (e.g., "Check Eligibility Now").

### Inputs & Selector Fields

- **Segmented Search Bar (Flight & Visa Search Style):** Interconnected module with vertical hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Field Anatomy:** Label pinned above in `label-caps` (`#64748B`), bold value selection beneath (`headline-sm`, `#071B3B`), accompanied by contextual vector iconography (globe, passport, calendar).
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in Electric Cyan (`#00D2FF`) with a soft blue diffusion blur.

### Destination & Visa Cards

- Semi-translucent pure white body with high-resolution imagery masked by subtle top-curved bounds.
- Includes a floating security chip header displaying the country code, official fee, and average processing turnaround (e.g., "B1/B2 Visa • 3-5 Days").
- Hover behavior: gentle Y-axis translation (-4px) with cyan perimeter highlight.

### Chips & Trust Badges

- **Status Indicator Badges:** Pill-shaped capsules with a glowing status dot (Green for "Consular Open", Electric Cyan for "Guaranteed Review", Royal Blue for "31k+ Approved").
- **Filter Chips:** Capsule-shaped with light slate stroke, switching to high-contrast navy fill with white text when active.

### Checkboxes & Radios

- Custom rounded squares (checkbox) and concentric rings (radio) finished in Deep Navy borders.
- Checked state fills with Royal Atlantic Blue (`#2563EB`) and displays a crisp white SVG checkmark.

### Specialized Agency Widgets

- **Visa Fee & Timeline Estimator:** Interactive slider with immediate live total breakdowns, consular surcharge indicators, and document completion checklists.
- **Flight & Application Tracker Barcode Strip:** Distinct decorative passport data-strip with faux MRZ typography for visual authenticity and immigration agency credibility.
